Chapter One

The mind is always restless sometimes we get caught up between self-doubt and worry. Out of desperation I tried something simple I closed my eyes and counted each breath. Ten minutes felt like for forever but when I opened them I noticed something I hadn’t felt in a long time calm. That small habit became my anchor. Every day since I return to it by breathing in and breathing out reminding myself that even in chaos there can be stillness. Later I learned that this practice is a common practice in order to boost concentration and it is called“mindfulness meditation”. Therapists describe it as a complementary approach for stress anxiety symptoms chronic pain and general wellbeing. But for me it became more than an attention training technique it became survival. I usually overthink constantly second guessing myself but these moments taught me something powerful. Negative thoughts are like passing clouds and they are moments of reflection They shouldn't decide what I do, or define who I am instead they should lead to self discovery. Sometimes I feel like I am half human and half machine because of how deeply technology has fused with my life. The devices and tools I use every day make my work easier yet they also remind me how fragile my attention can be. The internet is always whispering and offering answers to questions I haven’t even asked, shaping the way I think what I wear and even what dreams feel worth chasing. But when I look back on the world I grew up in I remember a quieter time. My questions once came from books from long conversations with friends from sitting under a tree in stillness. Today, the digital sphere has become a constant companion and sometimes I wonder if it is too close. It is tempting to let it decide for me, because its answers are quick and confident. Yet I know they are not always true, and they are not a mirror of my soul they are only reflections of data trends, and algorithms. I always remind myself that not every answer I need can be found online,Some truths live in prayer and in the slow unfolding of time. The internet may be a tool but it is not my compass. My life cannot be reduced to search results and curated feeds. It is tempting to let the internet decide for me what to wear what to believe who to love and what dreams are worth chasing. It offers quick answers and in that speed it feels wise. Yet I know the digital world is not a mirror of my soul I always try to remind myself not to dwell too deeply in this endless stream of information. My parents always encouraged me to value something deeper than material success they shaped my understanding about consciousness and spirituality in a world that often feels restless and chaotic. Over time I realized that true strength lies not in what we own but in what we know and how we use that knowledge. My education has been a journey of both discovery and setbacks I began my secondary school at Nigerian Turkish International College Kaduna a place that shaped me in ways I didn’t fully understand at the time. The school demanded discipline structure and high standards but it also exposed me to different cultures and perspectives. Sitting in those classrooms surrounded by students from diverse backgrounds I learned that education is not just about subjects it is about learning to live with and respect difference.
