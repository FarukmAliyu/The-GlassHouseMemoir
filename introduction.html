Introduction

I'm a technology enthusiast whose journey has been shaped by curiosity, faith, education, work, and a deep desire to understand the world around me. My story is not one of a perfect or straightforward path. It is a story of becoming of learning through uncertainty, facing self-doubt, experiencing failure, discovering resilience, and gradually understanding that growth often happens quietly. Some of my most important lessons have come not from moments of achievement, but from the difficult moments when I had to pause, reflect, and decide to keep moving forward. My education has played an important role in shaping who I am. From my years at the university to studying Computer Science, I have come to see education as more than examinations, certificates, or academic achievement. To me, education is the process of developing the knowledge, discipline, and courage to contribute something meaningful to society. Technology has also become an important part of my journey. My interest in programming, data analysis, artificial intelligence, and digital systems has taught me to look at problems differently. I have learned that technology is powerful, but it should remain a tool for solving human problems rather than becoming a substitute for human judgment, values, and purpose. Faith has been another constant thread throughout my life. It has taught me patience, gratitude, compassion, and the importance of trusting the journey even when I cannot see where it is leading. When the world becomes noisy, faith and reflection help me return to what matters. My experience working in sales also taught me lessons that no classroom could fully provide. Meeting people, listening to their needs, facing rejection, and eventually finding success taught me about communication, empathy, persistence, and integrity. It showed me that every person has a story, and that meaningful connection begins with learning to listen. I also carry memories of the natural world I experienced while growing up. The changes I witnessed in the environment, particularly the loss of trees and wildlife, shaped my understanding of how closely human life is connected to the world around us. Those memories became part of my desire to think about the future not only the future of technology and education, but also the future of our communities and environment. The Glass House is therefore more than a collection of memories. It is a reflection on the person I have been, the person I am becoming, and the lessons I hope to carry forward. It is about finding stillness in a noisy world, holding on to faith during uncertainty, learning from failure, embracing knowledge, and remembering where I came from. I do not claim to have all the answers. I am still learning. But perhaps that is the most honest way to tell my story: as a journey still in progress.
