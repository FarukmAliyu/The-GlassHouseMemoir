Chapter Two

Life in secondary school was not always easy. There were moments of struggle that felt overwhelming, and times when I questioned if I was good enough. But the friendships I made there, and the teachers who believed in me, carried me through. They taught me that education is as much about resilience as it is about knowledge. After secondary school, I continued my studies at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, through the Distance Learning Centre, where I am pursuing a degree in Computer Science. Learning online has its own challenges. It requires more self-discipline, more patience, and a stronger ability to motivate oneself without the daily presence of classmates or lecturers. At times, it has felt isolating, but it has also given me the independence to learn at my own pace and the determination to push through on my own. Studying Computer Science has been more than just an academic choice it has been a way of aligning my curiosity with my future. Every course, from programming to data analysis, feels like a step toward building something meaningful, something that could help solve problems in my community. In this journey, I have discovered that education is not only about passing exams it is about preparing to make an impact. Looking back, Nigerian Turkish International College gave me discipline, ABU Zaria has given me persistence, and together, they continue to shape the person I am becoming. My education has not been a straight path, but every challenge along the way has been a lesson, every struggle a step toward growth. Knowledge is light, it guides decisions, shapes character, and gives us the courage to create change. I used to think that school alone would make me knowledgeable. But I have come to see that education is more than classrooms and exams. It is experience, practice, and persistence. It is the skills we gain when life challenges us to grow. School was never just about books for me; it was about the people and moments that shaped me. I remember sitting in crowded classrooms, the hum of voices mixing with the sound of chalk on the board. Some days I felt invisible, lost in my own self-doubt. Other days, I felt unstoppable, like I could take on any challenge. One teacher in particular, left an imprint on me. They did not just teach the subject, they believed in me when I didn’t believe in myself. I still remember the day I failed an exam I had worked so hard for. I wanted to give up, convinced that I wasn’t good enough. But instead of scolding me, this teacher said, “Failure is not proof of weakness; it’s proof that you’re still learning.” Those words stayed with me. They changed the way I approached setbacks, not only in school but in life. Beyond academics, school was also where I learned the value of friendship. I met people who struggled just like I did, and together we encouraged each other through exams, long nights of study, and personal hardships. Those friendships taught me that growth doesn’t happen in isolation we rise by lifting each other. Looking back, my education was more than grades and certificates. It was a journey of resilience, patience, and discovery. Every failure taught me persistence, every friendship taught me compassion, and every lesson whether inside or outside the classroom prepared me for the challenges I face today. Faith has always been the thread running quietly through my life. For me, it is not just about religion it is about the strength I draw from believing that life has meaning beyond what I can see. In difficult times, when my mind felt restless with anxiety or weighed down by doubt, prayer became my refuge. There were nights I whispered words into the silence, asking for direction, and mornings when I felt the calm assurance that I was not walking alone. I always believe that resilience is not only built from hard work but also from trust in something greater than myself. My mother would often say, “When the world feels too heavy, place it in God’s hands.” At the time, I thought of it as just another saying, but with every challenge I faced, those words returned to me. Faith has guided many of my choices. It has kept me from giving up when life seemed unfair, and it has helped me see failures not as endings but as lessons.
